Where the baseline comes from

Opening, facing-a-raise, open-vs-3-bet, SB-vs-BB and SB-limp charts start from the PokerCoaching.com full preflop charts (100bb), read cell by cell; every combo count matches the PDF legend. The PDF has no limped-pot charts, so the "Limpers" charts are a live-1/3 default written for this trainer.

No-ante adjustment (default). The PDF assumes an ante. Your games have none, so the blinds are the only dead money ($4) and live opens are bigger. Starting from the PDF, the trainer trims the weakest hands in each group (ranked by a Chen-formula hand score that also credits pairs and suited Aces; bluffs keep suited and Ace-high hands longest): opens 5% UTG up to 15% BTN; SB bluff-raises 40% and limps 20%; 3-bet bluffs 40%; flat calls 15% (40% from the big blind, which no longer gets the ante discount); 4-bet bluffs 40%; calls versus a 3-bet 10%. Switch to "With ante" in Practice settings to drill the original charts.

Sizing the trainer grades (no ante)

Open $12–15. Isolate limpers to $15 + $5 per limper ($5 more out of position). 3-bet 3× the open in position, 4× out of position. 4-bet 2.25× the 3-bet in position, 2.5× out of position. 3-bet and 4-bet sizes round to the nearest $5.
